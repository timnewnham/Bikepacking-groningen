What you're actually renting

A proper escape, not an errand.

This isn't a spare bike for the shed. It's a loaded tourer and a full kit, ready to turn a free weekend or a chunk of your summer holiday into a trip.

Pick a direction and go

No planning around your own bike being road-ready, no last-minute gear shop run. Book, collect, ride. A long weekend can start on a Friday afternoon.

Tent, sleeping kit, stove, bags. It all comes fitted and ready, so the only thing you plan is where you're riding to.

Where to ride

Local routes, two to three days

A handful of loops from Groningen and Drenthe to get you started. Exact tracks and distances get sharper once you book, we'll send GPX files and a short briefing.

Groningen → Drenthe

Drentsche Aa valley

Quiet lanes and gravel through the stream valley south of the city. Flat, sheltered, and an easy-going first loaded trip.

~2 daysmostly flat
Groningen → Wadden coast

Lauwersmeer & the coast

Open polder and lake edge out to the Wadden coast. Big skies, wide horizons, and a proper sunset at the end of the day.

~2–3 dayswind-exposed
Drenthe → Westerwolde

Westerwolde forests

More forest gravel and a bit more relief, by Dutch standards. The Outback 2 handles it fine, just expect a slower pace than the flat routes.

~2 daysforest gravel

Looking ahead

Building a bikepacking community in Groningen

This started as a way to get more people out on proper loaded trips without buying a bike and a full kit first. Longer term, the plan is bigger than rentals: a proper local scene around trip planning, group rides, and swapping route notes, and somewhere to gather around it.

Next

Group rides and meetups for anyone curious about bikepacking around Groningen.

Later

A small cafe as a base for trip planning and community building, somewhere to sit with a map before a trip and swap stories after one.
